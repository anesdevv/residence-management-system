# Hyperframes Composition Brief: RÉSIVO Mobile Ad ("How to Use the App")

## Objective
Create a polished vertical mobile app ad demonstrating how students use RÉSIVO on their smartphones.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: portrait — 1080x1920
- Duration: 21.5 seconds (30 fps = 645 frames)

## Creative Direction
- Tone: `polished` mobile product ad
- Format: Vertical 1080x1920 with high-fidelity phone mockup
- Voiceover: Kokoro TTS narration synced to each user action
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` ducked beneath voiceover

## Steps Walkthrough
1. Hook: "Moving into student housing? Forget the paperwork. Meet RÉSIVO."
2. Step 1: Student card ID intake & real-time roster matching
3. Step 2: Digital resident pass unlocked with Room 204 & QR access
4. Step 3: Maintenance reporting with photo upload
5. Outro: RÉSIVO brand lockup & sign in call-to-action
