# Brag Plan: RÉSIVO Mobile Ad ("How to Use the App")

## What is this app?
RÉSIVO is the mobile-first student accommodation portal for Cité Universitaire Tlemcen that digitizes the entire dormitory intake lifecycle: instant student card verification, automated room allocation, digital resident passes with QR access, and one-tap maintenance dispatch.

## The angle
A snappy, high-energy mobile video ad demonstrating step-by-step how any enrolled student uses the app right on their smartphone to get their room key and live in the residence without ever standing in a paper queue.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
High-energy grab: A student phone screen lights up with:
"Moving into student housing? Forget the paperwork. Meet RÉSIVO."
Voiceover: "Moving into student housing? Forget the paperwork. Meet RÉSIVO."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Step 1: Instant Verification (3.5s – 9.1s):** Student enters their card ID on mobile. Real-time match against the official university roster verifies admission instantly.
- **Step 2: Digital Resident Pass (9.1s – 14.5s):** The digital dormitory card unlocks with Room 204 (Block B, Floor 2) and cryptographic QR code.
- **Step 3: Maintenance & Photo Triage (14.5s – 18.6s):** Need repairs? Report a leak, attach a photo, and track staff resolution in real time.

## Outro / punchline (18.6s – 21.5s)
"RÉSIVO. Student living, made simple. Sign in today."
Brand window seal and call-to-action: "Cité Universitaire Tlemcen · Web & Mobile Portal".

## User flow worth showing
1. Enter student card ID → 2. Match verified → 3. Room 204 pass unlocked → 4. Maintenance photo dispatch.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Upbeat, dynamic mobile app ad with voiceover walkthrough and interactive phone UI animations.
- Interpretation: Fast-paced, friendly, and visually clean, emphasizing speed and ease of use.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (Portrait)
## Duration: 21.5s (30 fps = 645 frames)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0A2342` (Navy 900) & `#0A2F5A` (Navy 800)
- Accent: `#0D7180` (Teal 600), `#109D9A` (Teal 500), `#E5A853` (Gold 500)
- Mobile Frame: Sleek modern smartphone mockup with rounded corners and bezel
- Display font: Outfit
- Body font: Inter

## Share copy (draft)
Moving into Cité Universitaire? Here is how to use RÉSIVO to get your room assignment, digital resident pass, and maintenance dispatch in seconds.

## Audio direction
- Role: Background electronic tech groove ducked under clear narration.
- Voiceover: Kokoro TTS (`af_heart`), natural, conversational pacing (5 clips synced to each step).
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at volume 0.22 (allowing voice to cut through clearly).
- SFX: Tactile phone screen taps, match chime, card slide, and photo snap.

## Storyboard
- Scene 1 (0.0s – 3.5s): Hook & Phone Entrance. VO: "Moving into student housing? Forget the paperwork. Meet RÉSIVO."
- Scene 2 (3.5s – 9.1s): Step 1 — Intake & Roster Matching. VO: "Step one: enter your student card ID. You are verified against official university lists instantly."
- Scene 3 (9.1s – 14.5s): Step 2 — Digital Resident Pass. VO: "Step two: unlock your digital resident pass, with your assigned room number and instant Q R access."
- Scene 4 (14.5s – 18.6s): Step 3 — Maintenance Dispatch. VO: "Need repairs? Snap a photo and submit a maintenance ticket right from your room."
- Scene 5 (18.6s – 21.5s): Outro & CTA. VO: "RÉSIVO. Student living, made simple. Sign in today."
